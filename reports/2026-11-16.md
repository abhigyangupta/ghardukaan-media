## 3 key takeaways

1. **The funnel is stuck after the click, not at reach.** Facebook reach went up 56% and people reached /signup, but there were 0 sign-ups for the second week running (none since 31 Oct). More videos alone won't fix this. The signup page, or the trust between seeing a reel and signing up, needs a look.
2. **"What to sell" and money topics beat generic tips.** Wedding-season led on Instagram, and qr-standee and pricing-formula led on Facebook. product-description (a writing tip) was bottom on both channels. Seasonal selling ideas were the only topic strong on both channels.
3. **YouTube skipped six days, and social sent almost no website visits.** Daily videos for 10–15 Nov never went to YouTube, and the 16–19 Nov schedule doesn't include it either. Social referrals fell from 13 visitors to 1. Facebook reach doesn't turn into visits: 3 clicks, 0 recorded Facebook referrers.
