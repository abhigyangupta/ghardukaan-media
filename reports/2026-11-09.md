## 3 key takeaways

1. **Nothing converted.** Zero sign-ups in peak festival week. Facebook sends the most site visitors (9), but no one signed up. The link → sign-up step is where people drop off, not reach alone.

3. **YouTube and attribution are weak.** 10 Shorts got 18 views in total. We can't see which campaign drives visits without Vercel Web Analytics Plus, or without logging UTM parameters in Supabase `first_touch` (that logging already exists, but no sign-ups came in to test it).
